Hiranya Signal Monitor v2.0 checks every 9/21 cross on the SPY 1-minute chart from 6:35 to 8:15 AM PT and labels each one A+, NORMAL or SKIP, with the reason. You act only on A+ and NORMAL and follow the lines it draws.

Daily routine

Open SPY on the 1-minute chart; the panel sits in the bottom-right corner.

What each setup needs at the cross:

  • A+: before 7:00, range ≥ 0.40× daily ATR, price on the trade side of VWAP.
  • NORMAL: before 8:15, range ≥ 0.25× daily ATR, QQQ 9/21 agrees, VWAP agrees, 5-min RSI not yet confirmed (below 50 for calls, above 50 for puts).

Taking a signal

Enter at the signal candle's close and let the drawn lines manage the trade.

  1. Option: today's expiry, $1–3 in the money (delta about 0.60–0.70), bid/ask spread $0.01–0.03, limit order at mid.
  2. Size by risk: each contract loses about $30 at the stop (SPY ~$0.50 × delta ~0.6 × 100); $150 risk = 5 contracts.
  3. Follow the lines:

  1. Risk rules:
    • Stop for the day after 2R of losses.
    • Expect about half of trades to stop out; never move the stop or add to a loser.
    • With every cross now checked, a new signal can fire while you are still in a trade. Take one trade at a time and ignore new signals until you are flat.
  2. Paper-trade the first 30 signals and log each in R before using real money.
  3. Alerts: chart → Alert → Condition "Hiranya Signal Monitor v2.0" → "Any alert() function call" for the bot's webhook (JSON with setup, direction, entry, stop, T1, T2), or the per-setup alerts (A+ LONG, NORMAL SHORT, …) for phone. Set "Once per bar close".
